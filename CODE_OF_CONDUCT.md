# Community Code of Conduct

*Version 1.0 • Adopted 12 March 2024*

Ethereum Mist thrives when people of many backgrounds collaborate with mutual respect and curiosity. This Code of Conduct sets the baseline for behaviour across all project spaces—GitHub issues and pull requests, discussion forums, real‑time chats, conferences and any derivative channels.

---

## 1. Our Principles

We commit to a **welcoming, inclusive and harassment‑free experience** for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, neuro‑divergence, physical appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, age, socioeconomic status, religion—or lack thereof.

A healthy community is built on:

- **Empathy** — listen before reacting and assume good intentions.
- **Respect** — value differing viewpoints and experiences.
- **Collaboration** — prefer constructive engagement over conflict.
- **Accountability** — own your words and actions; correct mistakes openly.

---

## 2. Expected Conduct

All participants are expected to:

1. **Engage actively and authentically.** Help the project grow by sharing knowledge, reviewing code and filing useful issues.
2. **Communicate civilly.** Choose words that foster cooperation; be patient with newcomers.
3. **Seek resolution.** When disagreements arise, discuss them calmly or ask a maintainer to mediate.
4. **Respect privacy.** Do not publish private information without explicit consent.
5. **Mind the venue.** Project spaces often overlap with public forums; represent Ethereum Mist professionally.

---

## 3. Unacceptable Conduct

The behaviours below are **not tolerated** in any project venue:

- Violence or threats of violence, including encouraging self‑harm.
- Discriminatory language or imagery targeting protected classes.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal insults or ad‑hominem attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others’ personal data (**doxing**).
- Repeated, unwanted contact after clear requests to stop.

---

## 4. Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code, please contact the maintainer team at **conduct@eth‑mist‑team.org**. Provide:

- Your contact information (optional but helpful)
- Where and when the incident occurred
- What happened; links, screenshots or recordings if available
- Any additional context

Reports are handled confidentially. We will acknowledge receipt within **48 hours** and outline next steps.

---

## 5. Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

- Private warning
- Removal of offending content
- Temporary ban from project spaces
- Permanent exclusion from all project venues

Decisions are made by consensus of at least two maintainers and communicated to the affected party. Appeals can be sent to **appeals@eth‑mist‑team.org** within **30 days** of the decision.

---

## 6. Scope

This Code applies to all Ethereum Mist project spaces—online and offline—as well as personal communications made in the context of project business. Behaviour outside those spaces may also be subject to this Code if it adversely affects the community.
